# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code, not
people.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason and, where you can, an alternative.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Say "I don't know" when you don't. It is always cheaper than the alternative.
- Respect that maintainers' time is finite and that "no" is a complete answer to
  a feature request.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, in discussions, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a GitHub Security Advisory (which is private) or by
contacting the maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.
Enforcement decisions are explained to the person concerned.
